Australia's Shoebox Goldmine

How sports trading cards became a global market, and where Australia sits in it.
By Ashen Manilka, 25 October 2026

Where in the world?

Americans search for sports cards more than almost anyone. Australia scores 20 out of 100, behind Canada, the UK and New Zealand. A score of 100 doesn't mean the most searches. It means the highest share of all Google searches in that country.

Interest stayed flat from 2021 to 2024, then took off. The yearly average jumped from about 25 in 2024 to 76 in 2026, and searches peaked in June 2026.

Zoom into Australia

Australia followed the global boom almost week for week. Interest stayed flat for four years, then jumped in late 2025 and peaked in mid-2026.

South Australia leads the country, and smaller states like Tasmania and the NT search more than NSW and Victoria. These numbers are a share of each state's searches, so a big population doesn't automatically mean a high score.

Data sources: Google Trends, "sports cards" search interest, Oct 2021 to Oct 2026 (trends.google.com). Australian state boundaries: Australian Bureau of Statistics (via github.com/rowanhogan/australian-states).
